How it works in 5 steps

  1. 01

    Set up in Settings

    Enter your business name, opening hours, closed days, holidays and appointment length. Add your logo and your AI key.

  2. 02

    Add your knowledge

    Write the questions customers ask and your answers: services, prices, hours, policies.

  3. 03

    Share your /chat link

    Copy your receptionist link from Settings and put it on your website, social pages or messages.

  4. 04

    AI answers and books

    Visitors ask questions and request a time. The AI replies from your knowledge and books a free slot.

  5. 05

    Manage it in the admin

    See bookings and conversations, update appointment status and keep your answers current.

/chat · fictional demonstration data

01 · Visitor side

Visitor chat

The page your customers use to ask questions and book an appointment, on a phone or a computer.

  • Answers questions about services, prices and hours from your active knowledge answers.
  • Collects name, phone number, service and preferred date and time, then books a free slot.
  • Quick buttons for common questions: Opening hours, Appointment length, Required info and Book a visit.
  • Shows your business name and logo in the header and next to every reply. On larger screens visitors can resize the chat window.
What you set or update here: Nothing to install on your site. You share the link; the chat page runs on your own server.

/admin · fictional demonstration data

02 · Admin

Dashboard

Your front desk at a glance the moment you log in.

  • Four counters: today's appointments, upcoming bookings, new conversations and knowledge answers.
  • A chart of new website conversations over the last seven days.
  • Receptionist at a glance: share of conversations with a booking, completed visits, bookings this month, total messages and all appointments.
  • Your next confirmed appointments and the latest conversations, each one click away.
What you set or update here: Nothing to set. It updates automatically from your bookings and chats.

/admin/appointments · fictional demonstration data

03 · Admin

Appointments

Every booking the receptionist made, in one table you can search and update.

  • Summary cards: today, confirmed, filtered results and the current booking length.
  • Filter by All, Confirmed, Completed or Cancelled, and search by name, phone or service.
  • Each row shows the client, phone, service, date and time, duration and status.
  • Change a booking to Confirmed, Completed or Cancelled from the dropdown and click Save.
What you set or update here: Update each booking's status after the visit or when a client cancels.

/admin/conversations · fictional demonstration data

04 · Admin

Conversations

An inbox of every visitor chat, so you can read exactly what was asked and answered.

  • Lists each conversation with the visitor's name, status, last message, message count and time.
  • Search by visitor name or phone number.
  • Open a conversation to read the full history and the visitor's name, phone and last activity.
  • Delete a conversation and its messages when you no longer need it.
What you set or update here: Review chats before you follow up with a customer.

/admin/knowledge · fictional demonstration data

05 · Admin

Knowledge base

What your receptionist knows. The AI answers from the entries you keep here.

  • Add a customer question, your answer and a category.
  • Search your answers by question or answer text.
  • Edit any answer, or switch it between Active and Draft. Only active answers are used in replies.
  • Delete answers you no longer need.
What you set or update here: Add your services, prices, hours and FAQs, and update them whenever something changes.

/admin/settings · fictional demonstration data

06 · Admin · Settings

Business profile, logo and chat link

Make the receptionist match your business and your schedule.

  • Business name, opening hour and closing hour.
  • Appointment length: 5–480 minutes, in steps of 5. Bookings must end before closing time.
  • Closed weekdays and holiday dates. The receptionist will not book on those days.
  • Brand logo upload (PNG, JPG or WEBP, up to 1 MB), shown as a circle across the admin, login, landing page and chat.
  • Your receptionist link (/chat) to share, plus a Change password form.
What you set or update here: Set these once after installing, and change them whenever your hours or holidays change.

/admin/settings · fictional demonstration data

07 · Admin · Settings

AI provider

Connect the AI that writes the replies, using your own account.

  • Base URL, API key and model name for any OpenAI-compatible service.
  • The API key is stored encrypted and is never shown again. Leave the box blank to keep the saved key.
  • Save all settings stores the business profile and AI provider together.
What you set or update here: Paste your provider's base URL, your API key and the model name, then click Save all settings. The public demo uses the host's setup and never shows a key.

Good to know before you buy

  • There is no website embed snippet. Visitors use your /chat page, which you link to from your site.
  • The app does not send email or text-message notifications. New bookings appear in the admin dashboard.
  • You need your own OpenAI-compatible AI key for live replies. FrontDesk AI is self-hosted on your own computer or server.
